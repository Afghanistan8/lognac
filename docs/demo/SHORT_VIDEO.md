# Lognac demo under two minutes

[Watch the 1:58.6 video](Lognac-under-two-minutes.mp4) (1920 × 1080, male narration).

It introduces Lognac and the fictional UK discharge handover problem, shows the written review and optional evidence graph, explains the corrected amlodipine dose-change check, then covers Entire, cfo.ai and Neatlogs in that order. The Neatlogs timeout was deliberately injected. Lognac performed the retry; the hosted recovery trace preserves the failed span, so the video also shows the distinction from separate successful traces.

The exact narration and image order are in [short-segments.json](short-segments.json). The male voice was synthesized locally with [the voice script](../../scripts/synthesize_demo_voice.ps1) at rate 0; [the video builder](../../scripts/build_demo_video.py) assembled the screenshots and audio. The records and business figures are synthetic or hypothetical. No live clinical deployment, prescribing authority or complete safety-feed coverage is claimed.
